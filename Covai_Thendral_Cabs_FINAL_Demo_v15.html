Our Tariff

Indicative fare rules for planning your journey. Final fare is calculated using actual trip details and applicable charges.

Local Trip – Sedan & Mini

Sedan: ₹200 for first 5 KM; ₹30 per additional KM.

Mini: ₹200 for first 5 KM; ₹28 per additional KM.

Platform fee: 5%. Local personal-stop waiting: first 20 minutes free, then ₹2/minute. Traffic and road-block delays are not chargeable.

Local boundary is based on 25 KM road distance from Coimbatore Railway Station. If pickup or drop crosses the boundary, the outer charge is based on the greater outside distance: ₹50 up to 2.5 KM beyond the boundary; ₹100 above 2.5 KM. Charge only once even if both pickup and drop are outside. Above 65 KM actual pickup-to-drop road distance requires Outstation booking.

Rental Trip – Sedan & Mini

1–5 hours: ₹325/hour. 6–10 hours: ₹300/hour.

Included distance: 10 KM per booked hour. Extra KM: ₹18/KM. Platform fee: 5%. Sedan and Mini have the same rental tariff. The same 25 KM Coimbatore city boundary applies: if pickup or drop crosses it, add ₹50 up to 2.5 KM outside or ₹100 above 2.5 KM, charged once based on the greater outside distance. Outstation has no city-boundary charge.

Overtime for hourly rentals (1–10 hours): up to 44 minutes beyond booked duration is charged at ₹2/minute; at 45 minutes, the next applicable hourly slab is charged (the booked hours retain their original rate and the additional hour uses its applicable slab rate). One Day Rental overtime is confirmed by the office. One Day Local Rental: ₹3,500 for 12 hours and 120 KM; extra KM ₹18. Platform fee 5%.

Outstation – Sedan & SUV

Sedan: One-Way ₹15/KM; Round Trip ₹14/KM.

SUV (6+1 / 7+1): One-Way ₹20/KM; Round Trip ₹19/KM.

One-Way minimum billable distance: 130 KM. Round Trip minimum billable distance is 130 KM (combined Up + Down). If actual combined pickup-to-drop plus return distance is below 130 KM, charge 130 KM; if it exceeds 130 KM, charge the full actual distance. Driver Bata: Sedan ₹400/trip; SUV ₹500/trip. Platform fee: 5%.

Waiting for both One-Way and Round Trip: first 60 minutes free, then ₹2 per additional minute. Traffic, signals and road-block delays are not chargeable. Hill Drive Charge: ₹400 when applicable, recorded at trip close by Driver and reviewed by Admin. Office commission is 10% of Running Fare + Platform Fee only; Driver Bata, hill charge and waiting are excluded.

Tour Packages – Office Booking

Sedan: ₹2,000/day + ₹13/KM. SUV: ₹2,800/day + ₹18/KM.

12 hours per day. Toll, parking and permit charges extra. Any applicable ₹400 hill charge is recorded at trip close by Driver and reviewed by Admin; it is not selected during customer booking.

Advance payment: 10% of approximate bill through UPI QR (GPay / PhonePe). Booking is confirmed only after office verifies payment. Call 99941 20263.

Approximate fare shown at booking may vary from the final bill. Applicable platform fee is 5%. Route estimates depend on public map services and must be verified. Rental final distance is based on actual odometer readings; the route is only an estimate.

Important Policies

Key customer terms and service information.

Terms & Conditions

Booking requests are subject to office review and driver assignment. Fare shown before travel is approximate; final fare is calculated after the trip using applicable tariff and actual trip details. Customer must provide accurate booking information and follow pickup OTP verification.

If a customer or passenger damages or soils the vehicle, including vomiting, the customer is responsible for reasonable, evidenced cleaning and repair costs.

Privacy Policy

Booking details such as name, mobile number, pickup/drop details and trip information are used to process and manage the booking, coordinate the driver and provide customer support. Access should be limited to authorized staff and assigned drivers as required for the trip.

Cancellation Policy

Customer cancellations must be made by calling 99941 20263. No ₹99 fee applies before the driver reaches pickup. If the driver has checked in as arrived at pickup and the customer then cancels, a ₹99 cancellation fee applies. An unpaid fee may be carried forward to the next booking.

Refund Policy

No advance is collected for Local, Rental or Outstation bookings. For Tour Packages, 10% of the approximate bill is payable in advance through UPI QR (GPay / PhonePe). Duplicate or excess payments will be reviewed and eligible amounts refunded through the original or mutually agreed payment method.
